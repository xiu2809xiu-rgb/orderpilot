## What the briefing said

The full answers are in [the questions for the client](client/questions-for-client.md#answers), in our own words, and every assumption above carries its new status. The short version:

- **Both flows are the first priority,** with full accuracy first, then saving time, then fewer errors (A-20).
- **The SPR is a purchase request** from stock and the run rate, approved by any one of three in management through a coordinator, with a 24-hour email reminder (A-21, A-23, A-37).
- **Exact matching:** part number, quantity and unit price, with no tolerance and no partial shipments. A wrong invoice is reversed and reissued (A-26, A-29).
- **New asks, not yet all in the plan:** one searchable place for every document, an audit pack in minutes, a six-month forecast and a product-line health view, live Power BI reports and the assistant called Spark (A-38 to A-46).
- **AI:** said aloud to be unrestricted for the proof of concept, which is not the written permission our rules need (A-31).
