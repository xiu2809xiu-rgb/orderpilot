- **Status:** Open (not yet asked or not answered), Confirmed, Partly (some of it answered), Changed, Challenged (what Spectra said conflicts with it, and it needs a decision) or Dropped. [What the briefing said](#what-the-briefing-said) has the detail.
- **If wrong:** what we would have to change.
- **Q:** the question that settles it.

**New asks, not yet on our Must list.** One searchable place for all documents (a shared folder is acceptable), a 6-month forecast of sales volumes and PO-to-delivery time from history, live Power BI dashboards, and sales questions by brand. Copilot was suggested for analysing data and creating invoices or customer cards. Spectra's own priority order: full accuracy, then saving time without errors, then forecasting. Where these go on the backlog is for the team to decide.

**Facts worth keeping.** Short stock is the most common error, then wrong prices and price or cost mismatches. The purchase manager and the product manager are the same person. Some vendors need staff to place POs in the vendor's own system. Staff chase BL and AWB, check the draft, and courier documents through the bank to the customer.

**AI services stay as they are.** "No restrictions were raised" on cloud AI is not the written permission the [data rules](../CLAUDE.md) ask for, so a client's document still reaches no third-party AI service (Q-12 stays open).
