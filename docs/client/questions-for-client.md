## Ask for these first

1. **Samples of every document type,** with names, prices and bank details blacked out: two or three each of customer POs, PIs, invoices, packing lists, delivery orders, AWB/BL (one draft that needed fixing), import permits, vendor PIs and invoices, proofs of payment and **the SPR form**, with normal and messy cases. They go to `samples/` on one machine, never into Git ([how to try a sample safely](../data-formats.md#trying-a-client-sample-safely)).
2. **An export from Business Central** of the item, customer and vendor lists, or just its column headings if the values cannot leave Spectra.
3. **The timeline of one real order,** from the PO arriving to the AWB/BL closing: each step, who did it, and how long it waited.
4. **Twelve months of history** from Business Central: sales invoice lines, purchase order lines, items, customers and vendors, and SPRs if kept. One real mismatch and one amended PO, if there is one.
5. **The written line on AI** (Q-37), the next showcase date (Q-38), and the new last page of Spectra's proposal.
