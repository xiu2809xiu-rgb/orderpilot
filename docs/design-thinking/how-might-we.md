# How might we

Each question comes from a pain in [the journey maps](journey-maps.md) or [the problem statement](../problem-statement.md#3-pain-points). Each one points to the ideas we chose, and to the AI rules that limited them.

| #      | How might we...                                             | From the pain                    | The idea we chose                                                                                                  | Area | Ruled out                                      |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| HMW-1  | help Support enter an order once, without re-typing it?     | Re-typing every line             | AI extraction with confidence and source; Support confirms doubtful fields                                         | 1    | AI writing straight into Navision              |
| HMW-2  | let a new customer's layout work without a developer?       | Many layouts                     | AI proposes a layout mapping; a person approves it                                                                 | 1    | Hard-coding a parser per customer              |
| HMW-3  | make sure nobody has to ask who holds an SPR?               | SPRs waiting unseen              | Tasks for each role, with an owner, a deadline and reminders                                                       | 2    | AI approving small SPRs on its own             |
| HMW-4  | give the approver everything needed to decide in one place? | Requests without context         | The SPR page with its checks, stock and documents                                                                  | 2, 3 |                                                |
| HMW-5  | stop packing before payment, without slowing paid orders?   | Packing on hearsay               | The payment gate: a rule plus Finance's confirmation                                                               | 2, 4 | AI reading bank statements to mark orders paid |
| HMW-6  | catch a quantity or price difference before goods ship?     | Mismatches found late            | Fixed check rules with tolerances, and an exceptions queue                                                         | 3    | AI deciding what counts as a match             |
| HMW-7  | explain an exception quickly to the person who must fix it? | Time lost working out what broke | An AI summary of the difference; the logs stay the source of truth                                                 | 1, 3 |                                                |
| HMW-8  | stop a payment going to a changed bank account?             | Bank details on every vendor PI  | Compare with the Vendor Bank Account on record; any difference holds the payment for Finance and a second approver | 4    | Trusting the PI's details                      |
| HMW-9  | let anyone see where an order is, without asking around?    | No shared view                   | One tracker: status, owner, history, exceptions and overdue flags                                                  | 3    |                                                |
| HMW-10 | keep customers informed without more typing?                | Customers chase first            | Updates drafted by AI from the confirmed order; a person sends them                                                | 1, 2 | Sending emails automatically                   |
| HMW-11 | show an auditor that every control held?                    | Rules in people's heads          | Server-side policy, segregation of duties and a tamper-evident audit trail                                         | 4    | Controls that only hide buttons                |
| HMW-12 | know whether OrderPilot actually saves time?                | No baselines                     | The measurement protocol, manual against OrderPilot                                                                | All  |                                                |
