# Hypotheses

Each idea in [how might we](how-might-we.md) rests on a belief we can test. Each hypothesis says what we believe, how we will test it, and what result would prove us wrong. Results go in the last column, with the date and the evidence.

| #    | We believe that...                                                                                    | We will test it by...                                                                                 | We are wrong if...                                               | Area | Result         |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---- | -------------- |
| H-1  | Confirming extracted fields is at least twice as fast as typing them                                  | Timing the same synthetic POs both ways ([the protocol](success-metrics.md#the-measurement-protocol)) | Confirming takes more than half the time of typing               | 1    | Not yet tested |
| H-2  | Extraction reaches 95% field accuracy on the synthetic test set                                       | The accuracy harness against the ground truth of the synthetic documents                              | Accuracy stays below 95% on the agreed document types            | 1    | Not yet tested |
| H-3  | A confidence threshold catches most wrong fields, so few errors survive confirmation                  | Counting wrong fields that were above the threshold and not shown for review                          | More than 1 in 100 confirmed fields is wrong                     | 1    | Not yet tested |
| H-4  | Tasks with owners, deadlines and reminders shorten approval turnaround                                | Comparing SPR turnaround with and without reminders, in a timed session                               | Turnaround does not fall                                         | 2    | Not yet tested |
| H-5  | Fixed rules flag every quantity mismatch between a packing list and its PO                            | The planted mistakes in the synthetic documents                                                       | Any planted mismatch is missed                                   | 3    | Not yet tested |
| H-6  | One tracker answers "where is my order?" without asking anyone                                        | Giving testers five status questions and timing the answers, by hand against the tracker              | Testers still need another source for any answer                 | 3    | Not yet tested |
| H-7  | A server-side policy refuses every unauthorised action, even through a direct API call                | E2E #3: direct calls by each role without the right                                                   | Any call succeeds                                                | 4    | Not yet tested |
| H-8  | Holding every payment whose bank details changed stops misdirected payments without delaying the rest | E2E #4, plus counting held payments that had no change                                                | A changed account is paid, or an unchanged one is held           | 4    | Not yet tested |
| H-9  | Spectra's staff will trust AI drafts if a person always sends them and the source is shown            | Asking testers to rate trust before and after using the draft screens (Q-20)                          | Most testers would rather write from scratch                     | 1, 2 | Not yet tested |
| H-10 | The AI cost per document stays small enough to be worth it                                            | AI usage records: tokens and estimated cost per document, against the time saved                      | The cost per document is higher than the value of the time saved | 4    | Not yet tested |

The thresholds above are our first guesses. Spectra's baselines and sign-off (Q-7, Q-19) may change them.
