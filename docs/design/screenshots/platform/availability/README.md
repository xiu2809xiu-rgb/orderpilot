# Availability on the avatar: screenshots

Taken on 25 Nov 2026 from a Docker build of this branch at commit 6e57fb81 (the review site, `http://localhost:4472`, fresh synthetic data), in Microsoft Edge with motion on, at a device scale factor of 2 (the two phone pictures are whole screens, the others are crops). Everything is "Synthetic demo data". Every account was put back to Available afterwards.

Made by throwaway Playwright scripts that are not kept in the repository. Each picture is taken with the picker opened by the keyboard (Enter) and the pointer parked away, so a mouse is not in the shot; a pill is photographed once its growth has ended (`data-settled`).

Light and dark are two files each: `name-light.png` and `name-dark.png`.
